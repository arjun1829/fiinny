How to Use Power Plus

1
🌍

Soil Application

3 L / hectare

Mix with water and apply through drip or irrigation. Best at early crop growth stages.

2
🌿

Foliar Spray

1.5–2 ml / Litre

Spray evenly on leaves — morning or evening. Repeat every 2–3 weeks.

3
🧤

Safety First

  • ✓ Shake well before use
  • ✓ Wear gloves & full sleeves
  • ✓ Use safety goggles
  • ✓ Wash hands after
  • ✓ Keep away from children
